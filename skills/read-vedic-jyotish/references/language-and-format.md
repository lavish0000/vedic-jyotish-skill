# Language and presentation

## Choose the language

Mirror the user's language unless they request another:

- `English`: plain conversational English
- `Hinglish`: Roman script with natural Hindi pronunciation and respectful
  `aap`
- `Hindi`: Devanagari only when explicitly requested

For Hinglish, prefer:

- “ho sakta hai”
- “signal karta hai”
- “is phase mein focus badh sakta hai”
- “chart ke is pattern ke hisaab se”
- “yeh tendency fixed result nahi hai”

Avoid stiff repetition such as “placed hai” in every sentence. Explain a
technical term once, then use it naturally. Avoid inflated spiritual
language, fatalism, horoscope clichés, and vague praise that could apply to
anyone.

Do not expose operational language such as:

- “the script says”
- “the skill calculated”
- “browser mein calculation hogi”
- “traditional Hinglish reading generate hogi”
- temporary file paths or raw command output

## Full reading structure

Unless the user asks for a shorter version, use:

1. **Chart snapshot**
   - Lagna with degree
   - sidereal Sun rashi
   - Moon rashi
   - nakshatra and pada
   - current Mahadasha and Antardasha
   - calculation method in one compact line
2. **Overall pattern**
   - three to five connected, chart-specific themes
3. **Current phase**
   - current timing window, activated areas, counterweight
4. **Ten life domains**
5. **Birth-time sensitivity and limits**
6. **Evidence ledger**

A full reading should normally be substantial enough to give each domain a
clear natal pattern and practical expression. Prefer approximately
1,500–2,500 words over padding or repeated caveats. A focused reading may be
approximately 500–900 words.

## Domain presentation

Use natural prose, not a rigid form, but cover:

```text
Natal pattern
Practical expression or dynamic
Timing, only when supported
Balancing signal
Chart basis: concise facts [E2, E7]
Confidence: Medium
```

Do not display internal confidence as a probability. Say “Medium within this
traditional rule set,” not “60% likely.”

Use tables only for the compact chart snapshot or a genuinely clearer
comparison. Do not place the entire reading in a table.

## Evidence ledger

Map each visible alias to:

- exact evidence ID
- short calculated basis
- polarity, strength, and stability when useful

Keep the ledger concise. Do not dump the chart packet. The reader should be
able to trace major claims without reading internal JSON.

## Tone and limits

Lead with the result. Keep one short scope note near the beginning or end:

> This is a traditional Jyotish interpretation of the configured D1 Rashi
> method, not a guaranteed forecast.

Do not repeat a disclaimer in every section. Be direct about unsupported
questions, then give the closest useful supported reading.

Do not present a challenging indication as doom. Explain what increases the
friction, what moderates it, and what practical behaviour may help.
