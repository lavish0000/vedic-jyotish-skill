# Domain guide

## General rule

Use the domain's returned facts, insights, evidence, counter-signals,
confidence, and limitations. For a full section, connect at least two
chart-specific facts when available. Never infer a person's gender or a
partner's gender.

| Domain | Supported focus | Do not infer |
|---|---|---|
| Self | Lagna, Lagna lord, Moon, visible style, inner response, decision pattern | Diagnosis or fixed personality label |
| Career | 10th and 6th lords, Saturn, work environments, responsibility and problem-solving style | Exact job, employer, salary, promotion or success date |
| Money | 2nd and 11th lords, Jupiter, earning, saving and judgement patterns | Wealth amount, guaranteed gain or investment return |
| Relationships | 7th sign and lord, Venus, Moon, partner qualities, bonding and meeting context | Marriage count, exact looks, profession, culture, divorce or legal result |
| Children | 5th sign and lord, Jupiter, Moon, parenting, mentoring and creativity | Count, gender, fertility, conception or pregnancy |
| Family | 2nd and 4th lords, Moon, speech, roles, boundaries and emotional home | Certain family events |
| Wellbeing | 1st, 6th, 8th and 12th lords, Sun, routine, pressure and recovery | Disease, diagnosis, treatment or lifespan |
| Education | 4th, 5th and 9th lords, Mercury, learning and skill style | Degree, admission, rank or exam result |
| Property | 4th lord, Mars, Venus, residence, comfort and decision pattern | Purchase date, ownership, inheritance or legal result |
| Travel | 3rd, 9th and 12th lords, Rahu, local movement, long travel and foreign exposure | Visa, country, guaranteed migration or permanent settlement |

## Self

Differentiate visible Lagna style from the Moon's emotional rhythm. Explain
where the Lagna lord places identity and major decision energy. Mention
repeated activation of the 1st, 3rd, or 8th houses only when returned.

Avoid turning one sign adjective into a complete personality. Include both a
capacity and a pressure point.

## Career

Describe work conditions, problem-solving style, responsibility, autonomy,
communication, service, leadership, or specialization only when returned
facts support them. A planet or sign indicates a mode of work, not a single
profession.

When several professions could express the same pattern, describe the shared
work characteristics. Do not manufacture a job title.

## Money

Keep earning channels, savings behaviour, family resources, gains, and
financial judgement distinct. Explain supportive and challenging signals
together. Phrase suggestions as habits or decision checks, not investment
instructions.

## Relationships

Use returned `PARTNER_STYLE`, the 7th lord's house, Venus, Moon, and
relationship activators to discuss:

- broad partner qualities
- independence versus togetherness
- communication and emotional rhythm
- likely social or life context for meeting
- recurring compatibility tensions
- practical relationship needs

These are symbolic tendencies. Do not turn them into an exact face, body,
profession, caste, nationality, culture, number of marriages, or divorce
forecast.

When asked about divorce or alimony, do not assign a probability. Explain the
returned relationship stress pattern, protective factors, and practical
relationship safeguards. Legal questions require a qualified lawyer.

## Children

Discuss parenting orientation, emotional care, mentoring, patience,
creativity, and responsibility. The 5th domain is not proof of biological
fertility or a child count.

If the user asks “how many children,” state that this D1 rule set cannot
reliably determine a number. Give the supported parenting and creativity
reading instead.

## Family

Distinguish speech and shared-resource patterns from emotional home and
residence. Discuss roles, boundaries, care, privacy, and family
responsibilities. Do not invent events involving parents or siblings.

## Wellbeing

Discuss routines, workload, rest, recovery, and stress-management patterns.
Never diagnose, name a disease, recommend treatment, or predict lifespan.
For symptoms or health decisions, direct the user to a qualified
professional.

## Education

Describe learning style, practice, communication, mentors, higher study, and
skill development. Do not guarantee an admission, degree, grade, rank, or
exam outcome.

## Property

Discuss emotional meaning of home, residence preferences, comfort,
decision-making, and caution around major commitments. Do not predict
ownership, inheritance, appreciation, or a transaction date.

## Travel

Keep short movement, long journeys, foreign exposure, and permanent
settlement distinct. Multiple returned foreign indicators may support
exposure, but they do not guarantee a visa, country, or settlement.

## Focused questions

Lead with the direct supported answer. Explain the limit once when necessary,
then provide:

1. the nearest supported pattern
2. its strongest basis
3. its counter-signal
4. relevant timing, if returned
5. one practical implication
